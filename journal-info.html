Target Journal

Journal of Healthcare Informatics Research (JHIR) · Springer Nature

This paper's primary submission target is the Journal of Healthcare Informatics Research (JHIR), a Springer Nature journal whose scope spans healthcare analytics, systems, and human-centered computing. That three-track scope, and its track record of publishing survey-plus-case-study work, make it the best format fit for this hybrid of a structured survey and ten application case studies.

Why JHIR for this paper

  • Format fit. Its analytics / systems / human-centered-computing scope accommodates the survey + ten case studies better than a study-only or systematic-review-only venue.
  • Framework contribution. The paper leads with an organizing framework (a source→communication→model pipeline with a channel taxonomy), which maps onto JHIR's analytics track.
  • Human-centered angle. The studies mediate communication with patients and care teams (triage, handoffs, portal routing), matching JHIR's human-centered-computing track.
  • External-validity story. The synthetic-to-real grounding is presented as a graded validation ladder, with a real-text medication-question study as the transfer anchor.
